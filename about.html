About Noventra

Honest guidance, affordable options and a clear path to studying abroad.

Our mission

Studying abroad should be accessible and affordable for every student.

At Noventra, our mission is to make studying abroad accessible and affordable for every student, especially those with limited budgets. We aim to help students secure international university admissions through their own efforts, without relying on expensive agents or unnecessary consultancy fees. Through honest guidance, scholarship opportunities, affordable university options, and IELTS preparation, we empower students to navigate the admission process independently, make informed decisions, and turn their dreams into reality.

A Message from the Founder

Welcome to Noventra!

I'm Muhammad Shafay, founder of Noventra. My own journey to studying abroad came with challenges, misleading guidance, and a gap year. But it taught me one important lesson: the right guidance can change everything.

I founded Noventra to help students access affordable international education, discover scholarships, navigate university admissions, and prepare for IELTS without unnecessary complications.

My vision is simple: to ensure that no student has to struggle with a lack of guidance the way I did.
